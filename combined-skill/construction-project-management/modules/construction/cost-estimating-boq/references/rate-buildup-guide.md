# Unit Rate Build-up Guide

All figures below are illustrative. Replace them with the project's actual labour costs, supplier quotes and plant hire rates.

## 1. All-in labour hourly rate

```
Basic wage / month
+ Allowances (housing, transport, food)
+ Overtime premium (if planned)
+ Statutory costs (social insurance, end-of-service / gratuity accrual)
+ Visa, medical, insurance, PPE, tools (amortised per month)
+ Annual leave & air ticket accrual
+ Non-productive time (weather, idle, travel): typically 10–15%
= Monthly cost ÷ productive hours per month (e.g. 26 days × 8 h × 0.85)
```

## 2. Worked example: reinforced concrete slab C35 (per m³)

| Component | Build-up | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Ready-mix C35 | 1.00 m³ × price × 1.04 waste | material |
| Pump | Hire per day ÷ m³ per day | plant |
| Place & compact | 0.6 h/m³ × gang all-in rate | labour |
| Curing compound | 5 m²/m³ × price | material |
| Vibrators & power floats | per m³ allowance | plant |

Formwork and rebar are measured separately (per m² and per tonne).

## 3. Worked example: reinforcement (per tonne)

| Component | Build-up |
|---|---|
| Rebar supply | 1.00 t × price × 1.03–1.05 waste (plus laps if not measured) |
| Cut & bend | yard labour 8–12 h/t or bending subcontract |
| Fix | 16–24 h/t (heavy raft 12–16 h/t; congested columns 25–35 h/t) |
| Tying wire, chairs, spacers | about 1–2% of rebar cost |
| Crane time | share of tower crane per tonne lifted |

## 4. Worked example: 200 mm blockwork (per m²)

| Component | Build-up |
|---|---|
| Blocks | 12.5 blocks/m² × price × 1.05 |
| Mortar | 0.02 m³/m² |
| Mason + helper | 1 mason : 1 helper, 8–12 m²/day for the pair |
| Scaffolding | m² allowance or a prelims item |
| Lintels, ties, DPC | measured separately or as a % allowance |

## 5. Preliminaries: time-related vs fixed

| Fixed | Time-related (per month) |
|---|---|
| Mobilisation and demobilisation | Site staff salaries |
| Site office set-up | Office running, utilities, IT |
| Hoarding | Tower crane hire and operator |
| Bonds and insurances (% of contract value) | Security, cleaning, welfare |
| Surveys and permits | Temporary power and water |

Always reconcile time-related prelims × programme months with the prelims total. The same breakdown is the basis of prolongation cost claims (see the `variations-and-claims` skill).
