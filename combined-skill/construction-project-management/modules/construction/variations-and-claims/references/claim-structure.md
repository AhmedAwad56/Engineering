# Claim & Variation Submission Templates

## A. Notice of Claim (short: sent within the time bar)

```
Ref: [Project]/[Contractor]/NOC/[nnn]          Date: [dd Mmm yyyy]
To: The Engineer / Project Manager
Subject: Notice of Claim under Sub-Clause 20.2 [or NEC4 cl 61.3]: [short event title]

1. We refer to [event: instruction ref / letter ref / site condition] which occurred / of which we became aware on [date].
2. We consider this event entitles us to an extension of the Time for Completion and/or additional payment under Sub-Clause(s) [x.x, y.y].
3. This notice is given under Sub-Clause 20.2.1 [or 61.3] within 28 days [or 8 weeks] of becoming aware of the event.
4. We are keeping contemporaneous records under Sub-Clause 20.2.3 and will submit a fully detailed claim within the period stated in Sub-Clause 20.2.4.
```

Keep it short. The purpose is to protect the entitlement, not to argue the claim.

## B. Fully Detailed Claim (FIDIC 2017 20.2.4) / EOT submission

1. **Executive summary.** Event, clause(s), EOT requested (days, new completion date), amount claimed.
2. **Contractual basis (entitlement).** Clauses relied on, with quotations; notices given (dates and refs); compliance with procedures.
3. **Factual background.** A chronology table:

   | Date | Event | Source document (exhibit no.) |
   |---|---|---|

4. **Delay analysis (causation).** Method and why it was chosen; the baseline or update used; the fragnet; results; float; concurrency discussion.
5. **Quantum.** Each head of claim separately, with the calculation basis, rates and source records:
   - variation / additional work valuation
   - prolongation: site overheads (time-related prelims)
   - head-office overhead (actual, or formula with justification)
   - disruption / loss of productivity
   - acceleration costs (if instructed or constructive)
   - financing charges (FIDIC 14.8 for late payment)
6. **Summary of relief sought.** EOT (days) and total amount.
7. **Exhibits list.**

For events with continuing effect (FIDIC 20.2.6), submit interim claims monthly and a final claim within 28 days after the effects end.

## C. Variation proposal / quotation (FIDIC 13.3.1 / NEC4 62)

```
1. Instruction reference and description of the change
2. Scope: description of the work, with drawings or specifications
3. Method statement and resources
4. Programme impact: fragnet and effect on completion / Key Dates
5. Valuation: BOQ rates / derived rates / new rates build-up (NEC: Defined Cost + Fee)
6. Assumptions and exclusions
7. Validity
```

## D. Response template (Engineer / Employer side)

For each head of claim:

| Item | Claimed | Entitlement (accepted / rejected / insufficient particulars) | Causation comments | Assessed |
|---|---|---|---|---|

End with a conclusion that states either the agreement proposed under FIDIC 3.7.1 or the determination under 3.7.2, and the date. Note that under FIDIC 2017 the 42-day period for the Engineer's determination runs from receipt of the fully detailed claim (3.7.3).
